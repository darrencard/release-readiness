# Quote assistant 2.4

Illustrative test brief for Acme, an invented company.

## Landing brief

- **Problem:** Quotes still need hand edits before they go out, so the assistant saves less time than it should.
- **Metric:** Share of quotes sent without manual edits
- **Bar:** 60%
- **Better:** higher
- **By:** 2026-12-18
- **Non-goals:** No pricing advice. No change to the quote PDF.
- **Tokens per task:** 9,000
- **Price per token:** $0.000003
- **The line:** model call. The assistant drafts the quote text; prices come from the price book in fixed code.
- **Decides the next step:** Head of Product, Quoting squad
